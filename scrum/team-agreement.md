#### Meetings

**How often will you meet?**

**What happens if someone cannot attend?**

- Zoom meeting if possible. Otherwise, we’ll update them after the meeting.

### Communication

**What communication tools will you use?**

- Discord

**Which tool will be used for urgent matters?**

- Phone Number

**How will important information be shared with the entire team?**

- Updates on discord

### Response Expectations

**How quickly should team members respond to messages?**

- Within 2 hours from 11 AM - 9 PM. 

**What should someone do if they cannot respond?**

- Let the team know that they’re busy and they’ll respond to the message later.

**What happens if a member repeatedly fails to respond?**

- Evaluate their work on the project. If they’re falling behind, do an intervention. If that fails, inform a TA.

#### Respect & Professional Conduct

**What does respectful behavior mean to your team?**

- Acknowledge and consider everyone’s ideas.
- Don’t be aggressive towards others or use harsh language.

**What behaviors are unacceptable?**

- Aggression and the use of harsh language or mean comments towards another.

**How will you make sure everyone has an opportunity to participate?**

- A planning stage before the project begins to adequately assign tasks to every member.
- Meetings where we’re all present so we can ensure everyone’s busy with a task.

### Accountability

**What will your team do when a member does not meet the expectations agreed upon by the team?**

- Urge the teammate to contribute as expected.
- Help them with their task(s) if needed.
- If the teammate’s incompetence slows down progress, duties may be reassigned and the teammate will be contacted more frequently about fulfilling their responsibilities.
- If the teammate consistently fails to contribute to the project, the TAs will be informed.

### Decision-Making

- Decisions will be pitched by members while brainstorming is taking place (or if an idea occurs to them at another time).
- The idea will either be unanimously accepted by the team, or discussed.
- If the majority of teammates are onboard with the idea, and nobody has strong objections, it will be approved.
- In the case of strong disagreement from one party, despite the majority votes, their perspective will be considered. If they are unwilling or unequipped to perform the task they’re disagreeing with, duties will be swapped between them and another member.

### Conflict Resolution

**How will your team handle disagreements?**

**Describe the steps you will take to resolve conflicts within the team before involving the instructor or TA.**

- Understand the teammate’s idea & perspective through discussion.
- If there is only one teammate with a different perspective, the majority vote generally has their idea approved.
- If the teammate strongly advocates for his idea, the rest of the team may either accept it due to the insistence or he gets vetoed.
- If the disagreement becomes aggressive or hostile, a TA is informed.
- If it is an even split, discussions continue until one side compromises with each side attempting to convince the other of their viewpoint.
- **Worst case scenario:** If the even split carries on too long, we just flip a coin — best of 3.

### Anticipated Challenges

**Missed Meetings:** If a member cannot make a meeting they may join via a Zoom meeting if possible. Otherwise, we’ll update them after the meeting. Team members are expected to let others know if they will miss the meeting beforehand.

**Missed Deadlines:** If a member knows in advance that their work is already behind schedule and might not meet the deadline, they are expected to inform other members immediately so that the rest of the team can discuss and come up with an alternative plan to help this member or reassign the workload thus mitigate the negative impact on the schedule. Except emergency/contingency situations, if the member fails to communicate with the rest of the team and the project missed deadline because of this, this member will be held accountable. 

**Poor Communication:** team members are expected to communicate with the rest of the team on their contributions, meetings, and responsibility of assignments. They are also expected to respond within 2 hours and must communicate if they are not able to do so. If the member is unable to be reached with both communication methods after a decent time span then the TA will be involved.

**Scheduling Conflicts:** Team members are expected to work out a schedule that fits in with the majority of the team. In the events of scheduling conflicts, alternative ways of communication like online meeting or brief will be provided for those unable to attend. It is also the responsibility of the members who are unable to attend to reach out to the rest of the team regarding their questions/concerns.

**Disagreements between Team Members:** In the situation of an unequal contribution, a group meeting should be held for all the members to discuss whether the current workload distribution is fair for everyone, and if not the team will discuss how to reassign the workload. The decision can be made through consensus/voting that involve all the members. If certain members do not agree with the rest of the team, TAs/Instructor will be informed to make a final judgment.
